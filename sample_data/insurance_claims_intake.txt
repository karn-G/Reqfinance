FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION SOURCE — Insurance Claims Intake

The claims service shall allow an authenticated policyholder or authorised representative to submit a claim with a policy reference, incident details, and supporting documents.
The service shall validate that required fields and documents are present before accepting a claim for assessment.
Each claim shall receive a unique reference and a status visible to authorised users.
The service shall route incomplete submissions to a request-for-information workflow.
Claim documents and personal information shall be accessible only to authorised claims roles.
The insurer shall define policy coverage rules, statutory timelines, retention periods, and service levels before production use.
