FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION SOURCE — Insurance Claims Assessment

The claims workflow shall record assessment findings, policy references, evidence reviewed, and the identity of the assigned assessor.
Decisions to approve, partially approve, reject, or escalate a claim shall include a recorded rationale.
Claims exceeding configured authority limits shall be routed to an authorised supervisor.
The system shall notify the claimant when the claim status changes or additional information is required.
The system shall maintain an auditable history of claim status and decision changes.
