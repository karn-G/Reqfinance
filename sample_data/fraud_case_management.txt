FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION SOURCE — Fraud Case Management

The system shall allow an investigator to create or update a case linked to one or more transaction or customer-event references.
Access to case details shall be restricted according to assigned role and case permissions.
The system shall preserve a chronological audit trail of case access, assignment, notes, evidence references, and disposition changes.
Cases requiring escalation shall be assigned to a designated senior reviewer.
The system shall support reporting on alert volumes, case ageing, dispositions, and investigator workload.
