FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION SOURCE — Digital Banking Account Services

The digital banking service shall allow an authenticated customer to view balances and transaction history for accounts they are authorised to access.
The service shall require multi-factor authentication for sensitive account actions, subject to institution-approved risk rules.
The service shall record account profile changes, authentication events, and sensitive actions in an audit log with timestamps.
The service shall notify customers when account contact details are changed.
The service shall prevent one customer from viewing another customer's account information.
The institution shall define and approve session timeouts, transaction limits, retention periods, and availability targets before production use.
