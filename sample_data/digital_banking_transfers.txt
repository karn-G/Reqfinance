FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION SOURCE — Digital Banking Transfers

The transfer workflow shall validate the source account, destination details, available balance, and applicable transfer limits before submission.
The service shall show the customer a transaction summary before the customer confirms a transfer.
The service shall assign a unique reference to each submitted transfer and display its status.
Duplicate submissions shall be detected and shall not result in duplicate debits.
Transfers requiring additional review shall be routed to an authorised operations user.
Failed or reversed transfers shall retain an auditable status and reason.
