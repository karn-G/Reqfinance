FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION SOURCE — Fraud Detection Monitoring

The fraud monitoring service shall evaluate eligible transactions against configurable risk indicators and institution-approved rules.
Each alert shall include a reason code, event reference, creation time, and risk score or severity where available.
The service shall route alerts to authorised investigators based on configured queues and priority.
Investigators shall be able to record a disposition, supporting rationale, and evidence references.
Rule changes shall be versioned and attributable to an authorised user.
The system shall not automatically make irreversible adverse customer decisions solely from an unreviewed alert.
Thresholds, model validation, false-positive targets, and escalation service levels require approval by the institution.
