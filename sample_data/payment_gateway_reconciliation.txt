FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION SOURCE — Payment Reconciliation and Disputes

The system shall maintain an auditable record of payment authorisation, capture, refund, reversal, and settlement events.
The reconciliation process shall compare gateway transaction records with merchant and settlement records.
Unmatched or inconsistent records shall be placed in an exception queue for authorised review.
The system shall record dispute status, evidence references, assigned owner, and resolution timestamps.
Only authorised roles shall issue refunds or change dispute outcomes.
